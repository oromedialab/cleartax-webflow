GST 3.0: The Next CFO Agenda
for India Enterprises

Impact of the 57th GST Council Meeting on cash flow, business confidence and the future of finance

The 57th GST Council Meeting recommended changes to the law. For a CFO, the questions are more immediate. What could these proposals change in the cash forecast, the risk review and the way finance works?

Join CA Binidh Gupta for a CFO-focused discussion. Explore what deserves attention now and what must wait for the final legal text.

28th October 2026 · 11:00 AM IST

SpeakerBinidh GuptaGST Expert, ClearTax

Watch the webinar

The reason to attend

The Council has made its recommendations. What goes into your next finance review?

Picture the next review. Treasury asks whether refunds could arrive sooner. Tax has a list of credits that may become eligible. The reporting team is assessing return mismatches. Legal raises a notice spanning multiple financial years.

These proposals are not yet new entitlements or obligations. But waiting for implementation can leave a CFO without a clear view of the potential implications for cash, controls and disputes.

The useful question now is: What can we quantify and prepare without assuming the law has changed?

What we’ll cover

Five questions for the CFO agenda

Cash flow

Which refund proposals could affect recovery, and what should stay out of the forecast for now?

Credit

Where is ITC difficult to trace, substantiate or assess against the proposed changes?

Returns

Can finance see the exceptions behind the numbers reported in GSTR-3B?

E-invoicing

Which specified purchase flows could bring new work into AP and finance systems?

Disputes

How should proposed procedural relief and the multi-year notice validation clause inform your risk review?

What you’ll take back to your team

Better questions for your next finance review

Leave with a way to separate potential cash from cash you can forecast.

See which controls you can improve now, and which rules are still being finalised.

Identify disputes that need review without changing your position prematurely.

This is not a walkthrough of every Council recommendation. It is a discussion about the decisions a CFO needs to make around them.
