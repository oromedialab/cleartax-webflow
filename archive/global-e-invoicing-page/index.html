Our story

Where we're headed, and why it matters globally

ClearTax began with a simple mission — to simplify financial lives for Indians. Over the years, millions of taxpayers have trusted us, making ClearTax India's #1 income tax filing platform. Our journey evolved with indirect tax solutions, built to streamline compliance for India's largest enterprises and automate financial operations for CFOs.

Today, our tax and compliance solutions serve enterprises in 50+ countries across EMEA and APAC. With AI-driven innovation, we support the CFO offices of 3,000+ leading brands worldwide.
