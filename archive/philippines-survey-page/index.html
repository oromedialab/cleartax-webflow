Philippines BIR e-invoicing readiness assessment

See how prepared your finance function is for BIR's e-invoicing mandate under RR 11-2025.

~6 minutes 8 questions 4 dimensions
Phase 1
Wave 1 issuance
31 December 2026

Large Taxpayers, e-commerce operators, and businesses using CAS or CBA must issue structured JSON invoices with JWS signing. No slack left in the window.

Phase 2
Wave 2 issuance
2027 · timelines TBC

B2C businesses, exporters, and taxpayers under incentive regimes. Wave 2 details to be confirmed by BIR through forthcoming Revenue Memorandum Circulars.

ESRS
Sales reporting
TBC · separate BIR regulation

Sales data transmission via the Electronic Sales Reporting System (ESRS) and Permit to Transmit (PTT) is a phase separate from invoice issuance. Rollout gradual.

Maturity Scale L1Early Stage · Not yet engaged L2Developing · Aware, not actioned L3Progressing · Underway with gaps L4Advanced · Owned and on track
Section 1 of 4

Regulatory awareness & phase readiness

Q1
Section 1 of 4
Has your organisation been formally notified by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) of your e-invoicing phase and go-live deadline under RR 11-2025?
Q2
Section 1 of 4
How well does your finance and IT leadership understand BIR's technical requirements — the structured JSON format, JWS RS256 signing, Electronic Service Provider (ESP) accreditation, and the distinction between Permit to Issue (PTI) and Permit to Transmit (PTT)?
Section 2 of 4

Technical infrastructure

Q3
Section 2 of 4
Can your ERP, CAS, or billing system generate e-invoices in the BIR-required structured JSON format with JWS RS256 digital signing — automatically, or does it need significant development or third-party integration?
Q4
Section 2 of 4
Have you decided your compliance path — modifying your own CAS with a Permit to Issue (PTI), or subscribing to a BIR-accredited Electronic Service Provider (ESP)?
Section 3 of 4

Process maturity

Q5
Section 3 of 4
Have you mapped and tested e-invoicing workflows for complex transaction types — credit notes, debit notes, service billings, official receipts, intercompany invoices, cross-border transactions under JAO 001-2025, B2B flows, and future B2C?
Q6
Section 3 of 4
Do you have a documented plan for ongoing BIR compliance operations post go-live — EIS response handling, rejection resolution, reconciliation, 3-day transmission monitoring, data-integrity checks, and staff capacity?
Section 4 of 4

Organisational & strategic readiness

Q7
Section 4 of 4
Has your organisation secured a formally approved, fully scoped budget for BIR e-invoicing — covering ERP or CAS changes, ESP fees, internal IT time, testing, training, post-go-live support, and any CREATE MORE Act tax deduction considerations?
Q8
Section 4 of 4
How is your organisation approaching the strategic value of BIR e-invoicing beyond compliance — real-time invoice data for working-capital optimisation, continuous audit readiness, AP/AR automation, or finance transformation?
