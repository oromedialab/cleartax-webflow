Closing the gap between AI promise and finance reality

You’re trying AI in finance. So why don’t the results match the promise?

Who should attendFor CFOs, financial controllers, and heads of AP and tax at multi-entity enterprises.

AI is ready for finance. Finance data isn’t.

Every AI project in finance is 80% preparation and 20% answer. Teams spend weeks pulling data from a dozen systems and applying the business logic AI needs to read it. Then they repeat it all for the next project.

The foundation already exists. E-invoicing mandates structure your invoice data today. Compliance-only teams file it and forget it. Leaders build on it.

Three things hold AI back:

  • 01
    Messy data

    Invoices sit in PDFs, spreadsheets and ERPs that don’t talk to each other.

  • 02
    Output you can’t trust

    Generic AI sees only what you paste in. It lacks your business rules, GL structure and entity setup – and no figure traces back to a source record.

  • 03
    Dependence on IT

    Finance waits in the queue for every new use case.
