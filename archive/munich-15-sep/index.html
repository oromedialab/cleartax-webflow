The sequence

One mandate, then two more.

Each step looks manageable on its own. Together they mean a company that's "done" in 2027 has two more builds coming — and the EU-wide layer doesn't care how ready Germany specifically is.

Step one · the easy one
2027
B2B e-invoicing
What it is
  • Structured invoice data exchanged between businesses
  • No transmission to the tax authority required
  • Post-audit model — the administration isn't in the loop
The foundation for what follows — not the finish line
Step two · a separate build
~2028
Domestic e-reporting
What changes
  • Invoice data goes to the tax administration per transaction — not just to the buyer
  • Fixed transmission windows; today's batch filings won't be enough
  • A separate build, not an extension of the B2B mandate
Most roadmaps stop at 2027. This is the deadline that quietly doubles the work — its own scope, owner and budget line, still to be decided.
Step three · EU-wide
2030
ViDA DRR
Single market
  • All EU member states, all cross-border B2B invoices
  • Per-transaction digital reporting to the EU layer
  • Applies regardless of how well 2027 and 2028 were handled
Already supported by ClearTax

Why attend

Most 2027 roadmaps stop at 2027. That's the risk, not the reassurance.

A company fully compliant with Germany's B2B mandate still has a domestic e-reporting build coming in 2028, and an EU-wide cross-border requirement under ViDA landing in 2030 — two more projects most plans haven't accounted for, each with its own scope, owner and budget line still to be decided. This session is built for the leaders who have to answer for that gap: is compliance being built once, or three separate times.

The question on the table
Is 2027, 2028 and 2030 being planned as one roadmap — or three separate projects with three separate owners?

What the table works through

  • The false finish lineWhether 2028's domestic e-reporting is on the roadmap yet — or if it's still just the B2B mandate.
  • Reuse versus rebuildFor anyone already live in another EU market — does that build carry over, or is it starting again?
  • Central or local ownershipOnce mandates land across your markets — owned centrally, or country by country by 2030?
