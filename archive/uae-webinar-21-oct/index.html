Why attend

The ASP decision is made. Then comes the harder question.

Picture the 30 October steering-committee meeting. The provider has been appointed.

The main ERP may be covered. But what about invoices generated elsewhere? Are data issues visible? Is there a date for end-to-end testing, and someone responsible for resolving the issues it brings up?

30 October should be a progress meeting, not the first meeting about the implementation plan.
