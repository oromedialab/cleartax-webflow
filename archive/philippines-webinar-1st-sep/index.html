How to Get Your PTT & EIS Certificate after CAS/AC?

Get ready before the Dec 2026 BIR deadline

You registered your accounting system and received your Acknowledgement Certificate (AC). Many finance teams read that as the finish line for the BIR e-invoicing mandate. It is the starting line, and this session is how you get ready for what comes next.

With mandatory issuance due by 31 December 2026 and live transmission from 1 January 2027, the question is no longer "are we registered?" It is "can we actually transmit, and have we passed BIR certification to do it?"

CAS/AC is not EIS compliance

Computerized Accounting System (CAS) registration and Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) certification are two separate tracks. Holding one does not give you the other.

What you have

CAS registration & Acknowledgement Certificate

Your AC, issued under RMC 5-2021 and RMO 9-2021, confirms that your books and invoicing system are registered.

  • Registers your books-of-accounts and invoicing software
  • Sworn statement plus sample print copies of system-generated receipts, invoices and books
  • Says nothing about your ability to transmit sales data to the BIR
is not
What you still need

Permit to Transmit & EIS Certificate

A distinct and additional approval under the e-invoicing mandate, and a technical exercise, not a paperwork one.

  • Structured JSON signed with JWS (RS256)
  • Connection to the EIS APIs, cleared through BIR sandbox testing and a joint UAT
  • Issued to you, the taxpayer, not to your software vendor; so your team is in the room regardless of the tools you use

That gap is exactly where most Philippine enterprises are stuck right now. If your plan assumes CAS registration means you are done, this is how you get ready for the multiple steps still standing between you and compliant transmission.

What the session covers

1

CAS/AC is not EIS compliance

Your Acknowledgement Certificate registers your system. It does not authorise you to transmit to the BIR. Leave knowing exactly what your AC does and does not cover.

We walk you through the phased rollout and the head office and branch rule so you can self-identify in minutes. If one branch is in scope, every branch is.

Two gates, one sequence: certification, then PTT

Technical certification of your JSON, JWS signing, and API stability must clear before the BIR issues your Permit to Transmit. Know the order so you do not queue at the wrong gate first.

Sandbox testing, digital signing keys, and a joint UAT with the BIR stand between you and go-live. See exactly what your system has to prove.

Certification, testing, and PTT can take weeks; most teams have not budgeted against a 31 December 2026 deadline. Map the runway before it maps you.

Walk away with a readiness checklist

Mandate group, system JSON and JWS readiness, portal registration, certification testing, PTT, go-live. One takeaway your team can act on Monday.

What's required after CAS/AC, getting to PTT/EIS certification

1
Confirm you're in a mandated group

Stage 1 (mandatory issuance by 31 December 2026, live transmission from 1 January 2027) covers online/digital sellers and marketplaces, Large Taxpayer Service registrants, corporations with annual sales over PHP 1 billion, and anyone already using BIR-approved computerized invoicing systems. If your head office or any branch qualifies, all branches must comply.

2
Configure your invoicing system (CAS/CRM/POS)

Generate structured sales data as JSON, matching BIR's schema, with JWS digital signing and stable API connectivity to EIS/eSRS. ClearTax will help with this by integrating your CAS/CRM/POS systems. You'll be required to add your ASP while signing up for the EIS & PTT certificate, select ClearTax as your solution provider.

3
Register on the EIS Certification Portal (eis-cert.bir.gov.ph)

Submit company authorization documents, official IDs for authorized representatives, and your confirmed BIR registration/TIN.

4
Generate your application key and digital signing key pair

Generate the private and public keys, verify by email, then download the files and store them in a safe place.

5
Undergo EIS Certification

The BIR runs API testing on its sandbox and validates your transmission system, JSON schema compliance, digital signing, API stability. ClearTax will help you set this up. This is a distinct technical validation, separate from your CAS/AC paperwork.

6
BIR issues the Permit to Transmit (PTT)

Once certified, the PTT is your authorization to start live transmission of sales data to EIS.

7
Go live

Issue e-invoices and transmit sales data within 3 calendar days of each transaction, monitor validation and acknowledgment status, and maintain digital archives (with printed backups for years 1–5), records must be retrievable for 10 years total.

The readiness checklist you'll leave with

Mandate group→ System JSON & JWS readiness→ Portal registration→ Certification testing→ PTT→ Go-live
