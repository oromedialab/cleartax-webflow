Why this session

The problem with solving mandates one at a time

Most organisations treat e-invoicing as a series of local fires. A mandate lands in one country, a project team forms, a deadline is hit, the team disbands. Then the next country announces its own mandate — with its own rules, its own ERP quirks, its own reporting model — and the work starts over from zero.

For multinational organisations operating across the EU, GCC and Southeast Asia, e-invoicing has stopped being a compliance exercise and started being an operating model question.

That approach was survivable when there was one mandate. It stops working the moment there are ten.

Does every new country reset the clock, or does the organisation get faster each time? That question is what this session is about.
