# Logo

The cleartax wordmark is the primary and preferred expression of the brand. It is always set as a single, unbroken word in lowercase, in the custom brand typeface, and is used in full wherever space allows.

The lowercase is deliberate: it signals approachability and clarity rather than institutional formality — a company that removes complexity rather than adds to it. Never capitalise the wordmark to "Cleartax" or "ClearTax" in logo form, and never letterspace, condense, or re-set it in another typeface. **The wordmark is artwork, not text. Always place the supplied file; never retype it.**

## Construction

The wordmark is a single, fixed artwork. Never retype, resize individual letters, adjust spacing, or rebuild it. Always use the master artwork at its original **218 × 50 unit ratio (4.36 : 1)**.

The wordmark is one continuous word — "clear" and "tax" are never separated, hyphenated, stacked, or differentiated by colour or weight. The join is the idea: clarity and tax as a single thing. Its character is geometric and rounded, with even stroke weight, open counters and soft terminals, which is what gives it warmth without losing authority. Proportions, kerning and stroke weight are fixed and must never be altered. Do not add a symbol, icon, tagline, or descriptor into the wordmark itself; endorsements and product names sit outside the logo in an approved lockup.

Inside it, the proportions do the work:

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Master ratio | 218 : 50 |
| Ascender (the "l") | 48.5 units |
| x-height (the "c") | 33.7 units — 69% of the ascender |
| Rounded-letter overshoot | 0.65 units above and below the baseline |

The low, wide stance comes from that x-height. The "t" deliberately stops short of the "l", so the word reads as one shape rather than two peaks. The rounded letters c, e and both a's extend slightly past the baseline and x-height line — this is intentional, and keeps them visually balanced against the straight letters. The "l" sits on the baseline; the "c" is drawn through it.

## Clear space

To ensure optimal readability there must always be a minimum amount of clear space around the logo. This isolates the mark from competing graphic elements — other logos, body copy, image edges — that would crowd or diminish it.

Clear space is measured using **x**, the height of the "c". Keep at least **1x of empty space on all four sides**. Nothing enters this area, including text, lines, other logos, or image edges. Where possible, allow more.
